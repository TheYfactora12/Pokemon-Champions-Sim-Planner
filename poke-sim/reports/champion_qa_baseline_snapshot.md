## Source-Truth Boundaries

- Battle mechanics live in `engine.js` and generated runtime data, not directly in Supabase.
- Supabase can store approved source rows, teams, overrides, and audit history, but browser sim behavior must remain reproducible from the bundle.
- `verified` means an explicit local simulator regression or oracle case exists. `baseline` means metadata exists but dedicated behavior coverage is not enough for a 100% claim.
- Normal selectors should use approved Champion-legal teams only; custom teams still need legality checks before they can be trusted for QA.
